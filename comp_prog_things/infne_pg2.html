This page will focus on more homebrew elements of my campaign(s).

  • Crits are calculated with max damage die multiplied by 2
  • Situation Lawyering: During a combat sequence to limit arguments and speed up the sessions both the players and DM must get their point across in 1-2 sentences to argue for advantage on a check or a saving throw. This also applies to arguing for any useful edge cases with our other homebrew-made things.
