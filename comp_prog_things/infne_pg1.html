Party Roles

These are the general party roles that can help define or guide a party's composition and potential weaknesses.

Blaster
A long range DpS character that can deal notable damage to enemies from far away with spells. These chaarcters typically have access to abilities that can damage one specific enemy, like Eldritch Blast, or something that can deal with a crowd of them, like Fireball. The types of classes that falls under this archetype are Wizards, Sorcerers or Warlocks.
Controller
A character have several ways to control the pace of the battle through spells, manuveurs or features that can incapacitate, slow or prevent that enemies from moving easily. These characters typically achieve this by forcing a condition or altering the terrain around them in order for their party to have a better chance to attack the enemy. A controller-type caster often utilizes spells like Wall of Ice, Darkness, Slow or Hold Person while a controller-type martial class often utilizes feature like battlemaster maneveurs or bloodhunter curses/branding. The types of classes falls under this archetype are Bard, Druid, Sorcerer and Wizards. Alongside some martial subclasses.
Defender
good defend. Paladins, Barbarians, Clerics, Fighters and Artificer
Face
good speak-ability and roleplay. Bards, Paladins, Rogue (particularly those with Charisma Expertise), Warlocks and Sorcerers.
Healer/Support
good heal and good buff. Artificers, Bard, Cleric, Druid and Paladin
Striker
good 1v1 potential and burst damage. Paladin, Fighter and Rogue.
